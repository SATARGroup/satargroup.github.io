Research Thrusts

Neutronics

Deterministic and stochastic Monte Carlo neutron transport modeling across complex core geometries, flux distribution, and critical reactivity analysis.

Thermal Hydraulics

Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and two-phase flow modeling for reactor core cooling, heat transfer mechanisms, and thermal margin limits.

Multi-Physics Simulation

High-fidelity coupling of neutron kinetics, thermal-hydraulics, and thermo-mechanical feedback for accurate transient and accident analysis.

Microstructural Analysis

Investigating atomic displacement damage (dpa), radiation-induced phase transformation, void swelling, and embrittlement in nuclear reactor materials.
